Professional services

Nine services cover the financial and regulatory side of a business. Open any card to see what it includes.

Accounting & Bookkeeping

Records, classifies, summarises and interprets your transactions.

All four stages of the accounting process are covered: recording transactions in the books, classifying them into orderly types, summarising them into useful reports, and interpreting the figures to support decisions.

  • Online model: suited to small businesses that prefer to access their data online.
  • Offline model: suited to medium and large organisations, with accounting carried out at the client's office and timely data reporting.
  • A dedicated team of Chartered Accountants and experienced accounting professionals.
  • Standard operating procedures for accounting and tax functions.
  • End-to-end management of accounting, compliances and internal audits, including bookkeeping and invoicing.
  • Service on a monthly or quarterly basis, matched to the client's budget.
  • Tax calculations and filing of direct and indirect taxes, including GST.
  • Reports that help the client analyse revenue and expenses.

Financial Advisory

Strategic financial advice for companies with complex problems or disputes.

Comprehensive advice for companies dealing with complex business problems or disputes. The work includes:

  • Investment management.
  • Managing financial structures around fund raising activities.

Business Incorporation & Registration

Advice on the right structure, and the registrations needed to start.

Setting up a new business from the idea stage. The firm advises on the structure that suits your requirement and arranges the statutory registrations needed to run it.

  • Structures covered: proprietorship firm, partnership firm, LLP and limited company.
  • Start-up advisory: structuring the business, advice on the type of constitution that suits it, and the necessary registrations.
  • An in-house team of Chartered Accountants and Company Secretaries assists with business planning and the mandatory registrations.

HR & Payroll

Payroll run so employees are paid accurately and on time.

Payroll is handled so that employees are paid accurately and on time, with the correct withholdings and deductions, and those amounts are remitted on time. It includes:

  • Salary payments.
  • Tax withholdings.
  • Deductions from pay.
  • Payslips issued on time.
  • Payroll inputs such as attendance, leave, overtime, allowances, loan terms and travel claims, with reports generated from them.

Statutory Compliances

Keeps you compliant with the Acts that apply, by their due dates.

Compliance with the specified Acts takes time, resources and continuous monitoring as the rules change. It applies to every organisation, whether a proprietorship, a partnership firm or a company.

  • Statutory and legal compliances followed on a monthly, quarterly or yearly cycle, or as needed.
  • Helps avoid penalties and notices from the authorities.
  • The firm works as part of your team so you can concentrate on the business rather than the paperwork.

Goods & Services Tax (GST)

Registration, accounting reports, return filings and refunds.

GST is an indirect tax applicable across India. It replaced several earlier taxes levied by the Central and State Governments, such as VAT, Excise and Service Tax. The firm undertakes:

  • GST registration.
  • GST accounting reports.
  • GST return filings and compliances.
  • GST refunds.

Direct Taxation

Income tax filings, TDS compliance, tax planning and representation.

Direct tax work includes the following professional services:

  • Tax compliances.
  • Income tax filings for all assessees.
  • Appeals, revisions and representations.
  • Tax Deduction at Source (TDS) compliance services.
  • Tax consultancy and planning.
  • Transfer pricing.
  • International taxation.

Corporate Secretarial Services

Company law work under The Companies Act, 2013.

A company secretary assists with the following areas of business under The Companies Act, 2013. The firm helps ease a company's ROC compliances and works to keep filings on time.

  • Company incorporation.
  • Recording minutes of meetings.
  • Maintaining statutory registers.
  • Filing of various ROC compliances.
  • Filing of MCA annual returns for the company's financials and statutory matters.

Management Audit & Risk Management

An unbiased review of governance, risks and operating efficiency.

Management audits give the audit committee, senior management, head of operations and process owners a comprehensive and unbiased analysis of the organisation's corporate governance processes, risks and functional efficiency.

  • Suggests areas for improvement and added value.
